About

The story behind
the sound.

Music production is a passion project — something that happens at the edges of the day, before the world wakes up or after it goes quiet. It is not a career plan. It is not content strategy. It is just a compulsion.

It started with a classical piano background — years of Chopin, Brahms, and Bach before ever touching a synthesiser. That training left something behind: an obsession with harmony, tension, and resolution. The same vocabulary that makes a Brahms intermezzo devastating also makes a melodic techno drop land with weight.

The idea driving everything here is transformation: taking an existing piece of music — a classical work, a jazz standard, a song with lyrics — and rebuilding it as melodic techno. Not a remix. Not a sample flip. A complete reconstruction from the harmonic material outward, using hardware synthesisers and drum machines to arrive somewhere the original never intended to go.

The setup is centred on a Moog Subsequent 37 for bass and leads, a Roland Jupiter-Xm for pads and chords, and a TR-8S for drums. Everything is sequenced live-style through Ableton. The BPM range sits between 125 and 137 — deep enough to feel physical, fast enough to move.

Alongside the music, there is a genuine love of cooking. Not as a hobby complement, but as a parallel obsession — the same patience required to develop a bass sound over an hour applies to a seven-hour braise. Both reward you with something you could not have rushed. A selection of favourite original recipes lives on this site and is available as free downloadable PDFs.
