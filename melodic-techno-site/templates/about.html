About

Melodic techno,
rebuilt by hand.

Music has always been part of my life.

After sixteen years of classical piano, I knew how to interpret the emotions of Chopin, Brahms, and Bach. I understood harmony, tension, and release. But I never learned how to truly express myself. Classical music gave me discipline — yet improvisation, the freedom to create something that belonged entirely to me, remained out of reach.

So I stopped.

For years, I found another creative outlet: cooking. In the kitchen, there were no rules carved in stone. Ingredients became notes, spices became harmonies, and recipes became compositions. I could invent, experiment, and — most importantly — share those creations with the people I loved.

But something was still missing.

Music never left me. It was always there in the background. Eventually, listening was no longer enough.

I wanted to compose.
I wanted to create.
I wanted to tell my own stories through sound.

Today, that journey has become this project.

I take pieces of music that moved me — classical works, jazz standards, songs that shaped my life — and rebuild them as melodic techno. Not remixes. Not sample flips. Reconstructions: translating the emotions those pieces gave me into something new, something built for the dancefloor.

Every track is recorded live using hardware synthesisers, drum machines, and an Akai Force. Some instruments are analogue, some digital, but each has its own personality and imperfections. I don't work inside a traditional DAW — I perform the music, capturing it as it happens. Most tracks live somewhere between 125 and 137 BPM: deep enough to be felt, energetic enough to move both body and mind.

If this music resonates with you, I'd love for you to join this adventure.

Creating independently is a labour of love. Growing a community is what makes it possible to bring these tracks beyond the studio and onto a stage. If you enjoy what you hear, the best thing you can do is subscribe on YouTube — it costs nothing, but it genuinely helps open doors to future live performances and keeps this project growing.

And because creativity doesn't stop at music, you'll find another passion of mine throughout this site: cooking.

The patience required to shape a bass sound for an hour isn't so different from letting a sauce simmer all afternoon. Neither can be rushed. Both reward care, experimentation, and sharing.

Here you'll find a selection of my favourite original recipes as free downloadable PDFs, alongside free samples and sounds for fellow producers.

Take what inspires you.
Listen loudly.
Cook generously.

And if you enjoy the journey — subscribe and become part of it.
