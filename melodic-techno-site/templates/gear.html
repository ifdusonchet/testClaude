Signal Chain

How it all connects.

All hardware synthesisers and drum machines run into a Focusrite Scarlett interface via direct outputs. The Elektron Digitakt acts as the MIDI clock master, driving the TR-8S and the two synthesisers in sync.

Audio lands in Ableton Live on separate tracks. Each instrument gets a dedicated channel with minimal processing — a high-pass filter on everything that isn't the kick or bass, light compression to glue elements together, and the Strymon BigSky on a send return for shared reverb.

The philosophy is to commit sounds at the hardware stage. Very little is corrected in the DAW. If the Moog sounds off, the knob on the Moog gets turned.
