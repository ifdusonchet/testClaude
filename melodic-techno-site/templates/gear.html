Signal Chain

How it all connects.

At the heart of the setup, the Akai Force acts as both the performance hub and MIDI brain. Every synthesizer connects to it via MIDI and USB, staying in perfect sync throughout a session.

All hardware runs into a Focusrite interface via direct outputs, with audio landing in Ableton Live on separate tracks. Each instrument gets a dedicated channel with minimal processing — sounds are committed at the hardware stage. If something needs adjusting, the knob on the synthesizer gets turned, not the plugin.

The philosophy is simple: no soft synths as primary instruments, no shortcuts, no fixing it in the mix. What you hear in the recordings is what was played — raw, live, and real.
