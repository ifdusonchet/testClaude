The approach

The cooking here follows the same logic as the music: start from something understood, strip it back to its essentials, and rebuild it with care. No shortcuts. No substitutions for the real ingredient.

All recipes on this site are original — things cooked repeatedly over years until the measurements became instinct. They are offered here the same way the music is: freely, because that is the point.
