Music

Tracks & the stories
behind them.

Every track here started as something else. A classical work, a jazz standard, a song with words — then rebuilt from its harmonic skeleton into melodic techno. These notes explain what was taken, what was changed, and why.

Track 1

Rodéo — Dark House Rework

Source material: Rodéo by Zazie

"C'est la vie, pas le paradis…" Here is my electronic rework of "Rodéo" by Zazie — a song I've always loved for its raw, dark and powerful vision of life: you fall, you get back up, you lose control… and the ride goes on. For this version, I wanted to keep the strength and emotion of the original song while taking it into TheFAB universe: a groovy 128 BPM ride driven by massive analog basses from the Moog Subsequent 37 and Moog Matriarch, hypnotic synths and a powerful electronic beat. Dark, melodic, groovy and slightly out of control. Welcome to the rodeo.

128 BPM

Some songs are not just melodies; they are memories. A summer afternoon, smiles by the water, friends becoming family, and those fleeting moments that somehow stay with us forever. The light, the memories, the love, the feeling of belonging… It's all in you. Close your eyes. Follow the melody. Remember the people who made you who you are.

Track 3

Manitoumani — TheFAB House Rework

Source material: Manitoumani by M

Under a purple sun, where the sky melts into endless shades of violet, a tribe gathers. Barefoot souls, smiling faces, hands reaching for the light. Ancient rhythms rise from the earth while warm house grooves carry them into the night. This rework of Manitoumani is an invitation to let go, to dance without fear, and to celebrate the simple joy of being together. A journey where tribal percussion meets melodic textures, where friendship becomes rhythm and every heartbeat joins the same pulse. For a few precious moments, there are no strangers, no worries, no tomorrow. Only music. Only movement. Only joy. A purple sun above us. Friends around us. And the feeling that the world is exactly where it should be.

Track 4

Cortisone — Melodic Techno Rework

Source material: Cortisone by Fauste

"Cortisone" is my melodic techno reinterpretation of the beautiful song by Fauste, a talented young Swiss artist. With this rework, I wanted to preserve the emotion and vulnerability of the original lyrics while taking the song into a deep, cinematic electronic universe built around analog synthesizers, atmospheric textures, powerful basslines and hypnotic grooves. This isn't just a remix — it's a complete reinterpretation that transforms an intimate song into an immersive melodic techno journey. If you enjoy artists like Anyma, Tale Of Us, ARTBAT, Ben Böhmer, Massano, Stephan Bodzin or emotional melodic techno in general, I hope you'll enjoy this version.

Track 11

Wicked Game

Source material: Wicked Game by Chris Isaak

Wrapped in melancholic melodies and hypnotic rhythms, Wicked Game dances in that thin space where desire overrules reason and the heart willingly enters a battle it cannot win.
