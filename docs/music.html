Music

Tracks & the stories
behind them.

Every track here started as something else. A classical work, a jazz standard, a song with words — then rebuilt from its harmonic skeleton into melodic techno. These notes explain what was taken, what was changed, and why.

Track 1

Requiem in 128

Source material: Requiem in D minor, K. 626 by Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart

The choir lines from Mozart's Lacrimosa were re-harmonised on the Jupiter-Xm, stripped of their orchestral context and laid over a slow, relentless kick pattern at 130 BPM. The result keeps the grief of the original but replaces the church with a dark warehouse floor.

130 BPM

Track 2

Intermezzo

Source material: Intermezzo in A major, Op. 118 No. 2 by Johannes Brahms

Brahms' most intimate piano work, played at half its original tempo on the Moog and transposed down a fifth. The melody becomes something slow and hypnotic against a kick at 127 BPM with heavy sidechain compression on the pads.

127 BPM

melodic piano hypnotic

Track 3

Blue in Green (Dub)

Source material: Blue in Green by Miles Davis

The original trumpet melody rewritten for Moog lead. The jazz harmony was preserved but the time signature flattened into a four-four grid at 125 BPM. Deep reverb and tape-delay saturate the lead until it barely feels electronic.

125 BPM

jazz-influenced deep melodic
